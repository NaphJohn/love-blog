## 0. What this chapter solves

The first six chapters covered stabilizing yourself, being seen, gender differences, communication, winning favor, and influencing relationships. But many stall at the last step: **they know it all, yet spend every weekend scrolling at home.**

This chapter drops "breaking out" from theory to an **executable layer**:
- Fri 18:00 off-work → Sunday, a concrete Zhongkelu/Zhangjiang schedule (modular, splittable/mergeable);
- How many ways to meet girls — not 5, but **12 categorized by scene**;
- Rough cost per play (¥0 to ¥200+, four tiers) so budget never talks you out of it.

Three principles underwrite it: **eq1 stabilize before showing up**, **eq2 put yourself in the right scene so the like-minded meet naturally**, **eq4 phrase "want to hang out" as "let's do it together" via NVC.**

## 1. Fri 18:00 → Sunday · Zhongkelu/Zhangjiang Schedule

Zhongkelu Station (Line 13) sits at Zhangjiang Science City core: Changtai Plaza, Legend Plaza, Bibo Rd cafe cluster, Zhangjiang Art Park, Zhangjiang Sports Center — all within a 15-min walk/bike. No need to go downtown after work; the local area works.

### Module A: Friday evening (18:00–22:00) · light ice-break
- **18:00–18:40** Walk/bike to Bibo Rd, grab a light meal (¥30–50) — alone is fine and natural.
- **18:40–20:00** Sit in a Bibo Rd cafe (Manner etc.) with a book or laptop — **a man comfortable alone is itself a high-value signal**, easier to be noticed than head-down scrolling.
- **20:00–22:00** Pick one: ① cinema at Changtai Plaza (¥45); ② board-game/murder-mystery at Legend Plaza (¥50–80, naturally 6–8 mixed, lowest ice-break cost).

### Module B: Saturday daytime (10:00–18:00) · main activity
- **10:00–12:00** Zhangjiang Art Park / Gaosi Rd run or ride (¥0; a fixed route builds familiar faces).
- **13:00–17:00** Pick one: ① frisbee/flag-football rookie game (¥30–60); ② climbing first-timer (¥80–120); ③ book club / industry salon (¥0–30).
- **17:30–19:00** After the activity, ask one co-attendee "want a quick bite?" — **NVC: "really enjoyed today (observation), want to chat more (need), that noodle place downstairs is good, join?"**

### Module C: Saturday night (19:30–22:00) · deepen
- Live show (MAO Livehouse Pudong, ¥80–200) or board-game round 2; or turn Module B acquaintances into a "2–4 person small gathering" (hotpot/BBQ, AA ¥100–150).

### Module D: Sunday (all day) · out-of-circle day
- **AM** A recent 1-day line from Youxiake/Daocaoren (¥99–199 w/ transport) — mixed teams + all-day together, highest like-minded odds.
- **PM** Exchange WeChat on the ride back, message "great hiking today, hit me up next time" — no bargaining, done is won.

### How to split / merge
- **Local-only**: merge A+B as a local half-day (¥30–120), usable both Fri and Sat.
- **Serious**: run A+B+C+D (weekend total ¥300–500), high intensity but steady conversion.
- **Lazy start**: just A's cafe sit + D's 1-day line (¥100–250), lowest social pressure.

## 3. Cost Tiers (don't let budget talk you out)

| Tier | Per outing | Scene | Fits module |
|---|---|---|---|
| **¥0** | 0 | Park run/ride, cafe with a book, free exhibits, friend drop-by | A(cafe)+B(park)+D(free) |
| **¥30–80** | 30–80 | Light meal, cinema, frisbee, murder-mystery, book club | A+B full |
| **¥100–200** | 100–200 | Climbing, livehouse, 1-day hike, 2–4 hotpot AA | B+C+D |
| **¥200+** | 200+ | Music festival, nice dinner date, weekend trip w/ transport | C+D upgrade |

**Save smart**: concentrate "high spend" on D (1-day line / small gathering) for conversion; keep daily A/B at ¥0–80 for frequency. **Frequency > single spend** — showing up weekly beats one big splash monthly (eq1's predictable small rituals apply to "being seen" too).

## 4. Underwrite with principles (don't just copy the schedule)

- **eq1 stable frame**: before going out ask "am I steady today" — if not, recharge home, don't force a social.
- **eq2 right scene**: prefer the ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ rows (charity/friends), densest like-minded.
- **eq4 NVC request**: invite as "let's…", not "are you single"; on rejection, don't pry, leave the door "next time."
